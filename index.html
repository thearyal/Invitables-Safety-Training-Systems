Technical Implementation FAQ

How do you capture warehouses for a 360° interactive twin?

We use a combination of high-resolution photogrammetry and LiDAR scanning. A typical distribution center can be captured in 1–2 days with minimal operational disruption.

Does Spot the Hazard integrate with Workday, SAP, or Cornerstone?

Yes. We provide SSO (SAML/OIDC), SCIM provisioning, and learning-record exports that plug into major LMS and HCM platforms.

What is the pilot rollout timeline?

Most enterprise pilots go live within 3–4 weeks of signed agreement, including facility capture, twin build, and first cohort training.
